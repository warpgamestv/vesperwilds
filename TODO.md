# Vesper Wilds Mod Expansion Ideas

## 🛡️ 1. Vesperite Armor Set & Velvet Cloak
*   **Vesperite Armor:** A new armor set crafted from Vesperite Ingots.
    *   *Set Bonus:* Wearing the full set could amplify the `Twilight Resonance` mechanic (e.g., doubling the mining/attack bonus in dark areas) or provide a permanent "Night Vision" effect when your light level drops below 7.
*   **Velvet Cloak:** Crafted using string/silk dropped by the **Velvet Moth**. Goes in the chestplate slot (or a Trinket/Curios slot if supported) and grants slow-falling or a temporary hover ability, mimicking a moth.

## 🦋 2. Expanding the Velvet Moth & Gloom Stalker
*   **Moth Silk / Velvet Fuzz:** A new drop from the Velvet Moth (can be sheared or dropped naturally). It can be used to craft softer, decorative blocks (Velvet Carpets) or the aforementioned Velvet Cloak.
*   **Gloom Drops:** Have the **Gloom Stalker** drop something like "Gloom Essence." This could be used to craft a **Gloom Bow**—a ranged weapon that shoots arrows with a twilight-purple particle trail and inflicts a brief blindness or slowness effect on targets.
*   **Moth Attractor / Nectar Block:** A block crafted with `Glint Berries` that attracts Velvet Moths to group around it, making them easier to farm or keep around your base.

## 🌿 3. New Flora & Environment Blocks
*   **Luminous Vines (or Vesper Vines):** Glowing vines that hang from the canopy of Velvet Trees, emitting a soft purple/blue light (light level 10). They can be climbed and harvested.
*   **Ember Shelf Stew / Potion:** Since you already have `Ember Shelf Fungus` and `Vesper Sprouts`, these could be used in a brewing stand.
    *   *Potion of Resonance:* Brewed using Ember Shelf Fungus. Temporarily grants the player "Twilight Resonance" bonuses regardless of the tool they are holding.
*   **Vesper Glass:** (TODO from previous chat!) Vesper Glass blocks out all light completely (like tinted glass) *but* allows players to see through it perfectly, creating perfect dark rooms that look great from the outside.

## ⚙️ 4. Utility & Magic Items
*   **Vesperite Shears:** A unique pair of shears that have inherent "Fortune" or "Silk Touch" when harvesting `Velvet Ferns` or leaf blocks, netting you more saplings or special drops.
*   **Twilight Chime:** Crafted with Vesperite Nuggets and a stick. When rung, it pacifies nearby Gloom Stalkers for 30 seconds or makes nearby hidden ores emit a brief purple spark.
*   **Bottled Moth Lanterns:** An upgrade to the `vesperite_lantern`. Crafted by combining a `bottled_moth` with a lantern. It periodically pulses with light and occasionally repels hostile mobs.

## 🗺️ 5. World Generation Expansion
*   **Ruined Vesper Altars:** Small naturally generating structures made of `Vesper Stone Bricks`. They could contain a spawner for Gloom Stalkers and a chest with Vesperite Nuggets, Glint Berries, and Bottled Moths.

## 🌙 6. Mechanics & Enchantments
*   [x] **Enchantment: Penumbra:** A weapon enchantment where your attack damage increases the lower the surrounding light level is. Mutually exclusive with Sharpness, Smite, and Bane of Arthropods. Allows Vesperite weapons to become incredibly powerful in the dark when combined with `Twilight Resonance`.
*   **Shadow-Step Boots:** Boots that allow the player to move 20% faster when walking on blocks that are in deep shadow (light level 0-4).

## 🔮 7. Magical Items
*   **Star-Catcher Jar:** A block you place outside. Under the open night sky, it slowly fills with "Twilight Dew." You can harvest the dew with glass bottles. Drinking it provides a brief burst of levitation (like being pulled to the stars) and clears negative potion effects.

## 🌌 8. Events
*   **Event: The Velvet Eclipse:** A rare, random weather event (like a thunderstorm). The sky turns a deep, bruised purple, and surface light levels drop significantly. During this event, crop growth stops, Gloom Stalkers can spawn on the surface, but Vesperite tools receive a massive, temporary power surge.
